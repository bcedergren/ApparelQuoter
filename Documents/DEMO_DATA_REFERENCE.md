### Embroidery
- Cost per 1000 stitches: $0.15
- Hooping Fee: $25.00
- First 5000 stitches: $0.20 per 1000

### Pre-Cut Vinyl
- Names: $5.00 - $2.75 (based on quantity)
- Numbers: $3.00 - $1.75 (based on quantity)

### Wholesale Markup
- Less than 50 pieces: 100% markup or $5.00 minimum
- 50-144 pieces: 75% markup or $3.50 minimum
- Over 144 pieces: 50% markup or $2.00 minimum
